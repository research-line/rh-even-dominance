================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================================
If 2 S_n is non-zero, the cos- and sin-sector diagonals genuinely differ
in the correct Connes-vS form -> Even Dominance can be a non-trivial
question whose answer depends on the sign and magnitude of S_n.

If S_n > 0 for the minimum-eigenvalue mode, then Cos diag < Sin diag at
that mode, supporting Even Dominance asymptotically.
